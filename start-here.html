First Steps

Start here before touching anything else.

1. Stay calm

Most problems look worse than they are. Panic leads to bad clicks and worse outcomes.

2. Stop interacting with suspicious screens

If something looks wrong (popups, fake alerts), stop clicking immediately.

3. Disconnect if needed

Pull the network if you suspect something malicious. You can always reconnect later.

Mindset

This matters more than tools.

The goal is not to memorize commands. The goal is to understand what you’re doing and why. Slow is smooth. Smooth becomes fast.
